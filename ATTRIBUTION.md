## Wiktionary extract

`data/raw/wiktionary_hiligaynon.csv` is derived from English Wiktionary pages in the Hiligaynon lemma categories.

- Source: https://en.wiktionary.org/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0)
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/

Derived files in `data/processed/` inherit the same CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

Wiktionary entries were collected with a category crawl of Hiligaynon lemmas. The scrape is imperfect; the cleaning pipeline documents what was removed.

## What is not included

This public repository does **not** redistribute:

- Kaufmann's Visayan-English Dictionary extracts
- Motus / Motum dictionary or lesson PDF extracts
- PinoyDictionary scrapes

Those sources were inspected locally while exploring the language, but their licenses are not clear enough to republish here.
